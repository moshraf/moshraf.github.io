# Hi! I'm Moshraf Hossain

I'm a **GIS and Data Analyst** with a background in geology, coal exploration and geotechnical laboratory testing. Working with coordinate-based field data showed me that where something happens often explains why it matters, and that instinct still shapes how I approach data.

When I moved into data analytics, I built my skills in Python, SQL, dashboards and automation while keeping GIS active alongside them, as a skill I use deliberately rather than a leftover from a former career. I completed a Graduate Diploma in Data Science at Western Sydney University in 2026, and I'm now studying a Graduate Certificate in Geospatial Intelligence at Curtin University.

Today I design interactive dashboards, build automated data pipelines, and apply spatial analysis where location genuinely changes the answer — turning raw, messy data into insights an organisation can actually act on.
